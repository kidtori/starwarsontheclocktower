# Racing sources and scope

Researched on 2026-10-02 using official Star Wars and Galactic Racer publications. `2026-10-02/*.json` retains URL, title, access date and source HTML checksum, plus Databank metadata where supplied. Full biographies and artwork are not copied.

The [official game site](https://starwarsgalacticracer.com/) and [StarWars.com story announcement](https://www.starwars.com/news/star-wars-galactic-racer-story-trailer) identify Shade, Hibi, Kestar Bool, Darius Pax and Sebulba. They are the five confirmed game identities in this catalogue, including support characters rather than claiming all five are playable racers. Main Star Wars records represent each person once; `data/galactic-racer/characters` attaches sourced game context. Individual assigned vehicles, unrevealed rivals, performance statistics and post-launch outcomes are not inferred.

The [developer's handling article](https://starwarsgalacticracer.com/news/feel-the-speed-game-feel-in-star-wars-galactic-racer/) supports four vehicle classes: landspeeders, speeder bikes, skim speeders and podracers. The [edition announcement](https://starwarsgalacticracer.com/news/official-gameplay-trailer/) names the Kor Sarun Darc X, Ciza T and Rac S models. These seven game class/model records are separate from the five sourced film vehicles: Anakin's and Sebulba's pods, Rey's speeder, Han's landspeeder, and Enfys Nest's swoop.

The classic podracer group includes Anakin/Vader, Sebulba, Gasgano, Ben Quadinaros, Teemto Pagalies, Ratts Tyerell, Mawhonic, Ody Mandrell, Clegg Holdfast, Ark “Bumpy” Roose, Neva Kee and Dud Bolt. Most have individual Databank sources. Ark and Neva use the [Lucasfilm Story Group's podracing article](https://www.starwars.com/news/star-wars-inside-intel-podracing), while Dud uses [StarWars.com's character-name feature](https://www.starwars.com/news/25-weird-star-wars-character-names). The collection is source-backed coverage of twelve classic pilots, not the complete eighteen-person Boonta grid or a claim that those film pilots appear in Galactic Racer. Han, Rey and Enfys are labelled film speeder pilots, not confirmed competitive racers in the new game.

`node scripts/fetch-racing-sources.js` retrieves missing metadata. `node scripts/import-racing.js` validates and adds the racing supplement, backing up main and racing records first. It also refreshes `data/racing-catalogue.json` and the main catalogue count. Rebuilding the core Star Wars seed replaces generated core fields; rerun the racing importer afterward to reattach racing profiles. Both importers leave saved projects unchanged. Character importance, narrative alignment and BOTC casting themes remain labelled editorial drafts.
